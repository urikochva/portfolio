Barbell Bros

A marketing campaign for a gym, with a sign-up site built on dark UX patterns.

Barbell Bros is a Tel Aviv gym, “lifting together since 1996”. The campaign sets out to make the brand stand out. Its sign-up site leans on dark UX principles, a Gantt chart times the launch and each message, and a brochure shows off the gym and its new membership plans.

Type
Marketing campaign, web UI/UX, dark UX
Deliverables
Sign-up site prototype, brochure, social media plan
Tools
Illustrator, Photoshop, Figma and AI tools

Three dark patterns

“Most popular” badge
On the home page, the middle plan gets an eye-catching badge in its top corner. It tells people which plan most members pick and nudges them to pick it too. Its card is also bigger than the two plans that earn the gym less.
The longer commitment
The buy button on the plan with a commitment is bold and dark blue, so the eye lands there first and it feels like the natural choice. The no-commitment button is plain and quiet, steering people to the plan that earns the gym far more.
Opting out of ads
The “I don’t want ads and offers” checkbox is set in a very light colour, so it almost disappears among the other lines. Only people who read the page closely will tick it, and the gym can keep sending marketing to everyone else.

This was a course exercise in spotting how design can steer people. Building these patterns on purpose taught me to recognise them, and why honest choices, clear buttons and easy opt-outs earn more trust in the long run.
