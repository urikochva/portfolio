Don’t Mess With The Zohan

A family-friendly one-page site to stream the movie and shop its merch.

Fans have no single place to watch the movie or buy anything from it, and its grown-up marketing shuts out families. This site brings streaming and an official store together in one safe, cheerful space for parents and kids.

Type
One-page website, UX/UI
Deliverables
UX research, interactive prototype
Tools

02

UX

Pain points

Hard to find the movie

Fans have to hunt across different platforms just to find somewhere to watch it.

No merchandise

There’s no central place to buy real, movie-themed items and party kits to complete the movie night.

Not family-friendly

The film’s mature marketing leaves younger viewers and families no safe, kid-friendly way into its fun, energetic world.

Solutions

One-click streaming

A dedicated HD player for the movie, so it’s ready to play straight away without searching other platforms.

The official merch store

A one-stop shop for official themed merch and party kits, built into the same site.

A safe space for everyone

The grown-up parts of the original marketing are left out, so parents and kids can enjoy the movie’s energy together.

Usability

A short journey

A clean layout with few clicks. Users move easily between watching and shopping.

Help on demand

Built-in WhatsApp support gives quick help when something goes wrong.

Easy to come back to

Consistent patterns and a clear structure mean returning users never have to re-learn the site.

Quick to get

Familiar patterns that need no learning, so people spend their time on the movie, not the menus.

03
