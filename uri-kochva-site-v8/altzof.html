Altzof

A premium Israeli honey brand, from its brand book to a one-page website.

Altzof has made honey by hand since 1937. The project gives the brand a full identity, carries it across print, social and outdoor ads, and finishes with a one-page website for the honey shop.

Type
Brand identity, web design
Deliverables
Brand book, print and social, one-page website
Tools
Illustrator, Photoshop and Figma

Bring the brand online with a calm, premium shop page that tells the story and leads to the honey.
