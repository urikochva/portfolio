Grove

A calm money app for freelancers, from the brand to a working web prototype.

Freelancers earn uneven money from many clients, chase invoices by hand and dread tax season. Grove’s promise is “We make the complex stuff easy”: open it, understand your month in a few seconds, and feel calmer than before you opened it.

Type
Brand identity, web UI/UX, product design
Deliverables
Brand sheet, public website, web app: dashboard, cash flow, projects, invoices, expenses and tax jar
Tools
Claude Design, for fast design iteration

01

Brand: a weaving draft

Calm over control

Plain words, a “safe to spend” number and gentle status language. Feelings first, accounting second.

An automatic tax jar

A set share of every payment is put aside for tax, so tax season is never a shock.

A safety net for thin months

Grove works out a steady monthly pay: save the extra in good months, draw on it in thin ones.

Colour roles
Moss is the ground and wool the text. Orange shades are income, coral is always tax, and lichen means the safety net, so a colour means the same thing on every screen.
Typeface
Gabarito, a friendly geometric sans, set heavy for the big money numbers and headlines.

02

Public website

The home page leads with the promise and shows a real month straight away, so a visitor sees what Grove does before signing up.

03

The app

Each page opens with one big number and one calm sentence, then the detail underneath.

Not a bank
Grove never holds money. The tax jar and safety net are amounts it works out; the freelancer keeps the money in their own accounts.
Kept simple
No bank connections and one currency per account. Savings goals were designed and then cut, because they made Grove feel like a savings app.
Plans
Seedling is free with no client limit. Grove, at $9 a month, adds cash flow, the safety net, projects, expenses and an accountant hand-off.
