Chew Crew

A playful dog treat brand, from the brand book to the online store and a social reel.

Chew Crew makes treats from real ingredients for dogs and the people who love them. The project covers the full visual identity, the brand in use across print, social and packaging, an online store designed in Figma, and a short reel for social media.

Type
Brand identity, e-commerce UI/UX, motion
Deliverables
Brand book, online store, social media reel
Tools
Illustrator, Photoshop, Figma, Premiere Pro and After Effects
01 Brand book02 Social reel03 Online store

01

Brand book and visual identity

Vision

Treats made with real care, real quality and real purpose, turning everyday snack moments into something a little more special and playful.

Values

Quality, honesty, joy and compassion. Warm and approachable, never cold or corporate, like the bond between dogs and their people.

Logo

System
A primary logo with the wordmark under a grinning dog holding a bone, and a responsive version that uses the dog mark alone in small spaces.
Rules
Clear usage rules: inverted colours allowed, never stretched, tilted, flipped or recoloured, with clear space set by the letter E.

Colours and typography

Logo font
Dogbandy, a round, bouncy display face for the logo and headlines.
Text font
Futura PT Medium, clean and easy to read next to the playful headlines.

How the identity works across social media, print, merchandise and packaging.

02

Social media reel

Idea
A short, playful reel made to go viral on Instagram: a quick hook in the first second, real dogs, and a funny line that makes people stop scrolling and share it.
Format
An 8-second vertical video for Instagram Reels and stories.

03

Online store UI/UX

Click through the store prototype, built in Figma.

Prefer a bigger view? Open it in Figma

The problem
Most pet shops online feel cluttered and generic, and it’s hard for dog owners to see what actually goes into the treats they buy.
The solution
A clean store that carries the brand’s playful voice. Each flavour keeps its packaging colour so it’s easy to spot, ingredients are front and centre on every product, and the path from browsing to checkout stays short.
Screens
Home page, shop, product page, cart and checkout.
